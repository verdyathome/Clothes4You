# Refunds and returns

Refunds are recorded **on the sale**, from the item's page.

![Recording a refund on an item page](/guide-img/refund.png)

## Steps

1. Go to **Items** and open the item that was sold (or tap the item number on the **Sales** page).
2. Under the sale, tap **Record a refund**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Date refund paid**: the day the money went back to the buyer.
   - **Amount refunded (£)**: filled in with the full amount the buyer paid. Change it for a partial refund.
   - **Item came back?**: choose **Yes** if the buyer returned it. The item goes back **In stock**, ready to sell again. Choose **No** for a partial refund or if it wasn't returned.
4. Tap **Save refund**.

## How refunds affect the figures

- A refund **reduces turnover in the tax year it was paid**, not the year of the sale. So a sale in March 2026 refunded in April 2026 counts in 2025-26 and comes off 2026-27.
- A sale can only have one refund, and it can't be more than the buyer paid.
- The **Sales** page shows refunded sales with the amount and date.
