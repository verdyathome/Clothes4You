# Recording a stock purchase

Use this whenever clothes are bought **to resell**: from a charity shop, a car boot, a wholesale bundle, or even from Vinted or eBay. Each piece becomes a **Business** item with its share of the cost.

![The stock purchase form](/guide-img/purchase.png)

## Steps

1. Tap **+ Stock purchase** on the dashboard (or go to **Purchases → + New purchase**).
2. Fill in the top part:
   - **Date paid**: the day the money left her account. This decides which tax year the cost counts in.
   - **Total paid (£)**: the full amount, including any postage she paid.
   - **Bought from**, **Source**, **Paid by**: where and how.
   - **Receipt ref**: an order or receipt number, if there is one.
3. Under **Items**, add one row per piece of clothing. Only **Description** is required; brand, size, category and condition help later.
4. Tap **+ Add item** for each extra piece.
5. Tap **Save purchase**.

## Bundles: sharing the cost

Leave **Cost £** blank on every row and the total is **shared equally**. The line above the rows shows how much each item gets as you type.

You can also set the cost of some items yourself and leave the rest blank: the blanks share whatever is left.

> Example: a £10 bundle of 3 items with nothing filled in gives £3.34, £3.33, £3.33. If the dress was clearly worth more, enter £5 for it and the other two get £2.50 each.

If the item costs you enter add up to more than the total, the app says so and nothing is saved.

## Why the date matters

On the cash basis, stock counts as a cost **in the tax year it was paid for**, even if it sells the following year. So a bundle paid for on 3 April 2026 counts in 2025-26, and one paid for on 7 April 2026 counts in 2026-27.
