# Getting started

This app keeps the records HMRC expects from a sole trader selling clothes: what was bought to resell, what sold, refunds and running costs. From those it works out the figures for the **Self Assessment (SA103)** form.

![The dashboard on a phone](/guide-img/dashboard.png)

## Finding your way around

- **Dashboard**: the figures for the tax year, plus the quick buttons.
- **Items**: every piece of clothing, with its status: *In stock*, *Listed*, *Sold* or *Written off*.
- **Purchases**: stock bought to resell.
- **Sales**: everything sold, and the eBay import.
- **Expenses**: running costs like packaging, postage and mileage.

On a phone, the **☰ menu** (top right) holds **Help**, **Settings** and **Log out**. On a computer they sit on the right of the header.

Most pages have a **How does this work?** link that opens the matching guide.

## The one rule that matters: Business or Personal

Every item is either:

| | Business | Personal |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Bought **to resell** for a profit | Her **own clothes** she no longer wants |
| How it's added | **Stock purchase** | **Personal item** |
| On the tax figures (SA103) | **Yes**: counts as turnover | **No**: left out |
| On the platform check | Yes | Yes |

> Personal sales are still recorded because Vinted and eBay report *all* sales to HMRC. Keeping them in the app means the totals can be matched up if HMRC ever asks.

## The everyday routine

1. Bought some stock? Record a **Stock purchase** (see *Recording a stock purchase*).
2. Selling her own clothes? Add a **Personal item**.
3. Something sold? **Record a sale**, or import eBay sales in one go.
4. Paid for packaging, postage or travel? Add an **Expense**.
5. At the end of the tax year, **Download Excel** from the dashboard for the tax return.
