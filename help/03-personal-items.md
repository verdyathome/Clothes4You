# Adding a personal item

Use this for **her own clothes**: things she bought for herself and no longer wants. Selling those is normally not trading, so they stay out of the SA103 figures. They're still recorded so the totals match what Vinted and eBay report to HMRC.

![The personal item form](/guide-img/personal-item.png)

## Steps

1. Tap **+ Personal item** on the dashboard (or **Items → + Personal item**).
2. Enter a **Description**: the only required field. Brand, size, category and condition are optional.
3. **Date added** defaults to today.
4. Tap **Save personal item**.

The item is now **In stock** and can be chosen when you record a sale.

> Not sure which it is? If she bought it **intending to sell it for a profit**, it's Business: use a stock purchase instead. If she bought it to wear, it's Personal.

## Item statuses

Every item, Business or Personal, has a status you can change on its page under **Update status**:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In stock** | Owned, not listed yet |
| **Listed** | Up for sale; choose where it's **Listed on** (Vinted, eBay, Other) |
| **Sold** | Set automatically when a sale is recorded |
| **Written off** | Damaged, lost or given away. It disappears from the sale list. You'll be asked to tap **Update** twice to confirm. **Put back in stock** undoes it. |
