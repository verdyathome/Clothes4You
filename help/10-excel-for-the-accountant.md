# Downloading the Excel file

At the end of the tax year, or any time, download a workbook to file the return or give to an accountant.

## Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, choose the tax year from the drop-down at the top.
2. In the **Self Assessment (SA103)** box, tap **Download Excel**.
3. The file is called something like `clothing-accounts-2025-26.xlsx`.

## What's inside

| Sheet | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Turnover, each SA103 expense category, total expenses and net profit |
| **Sales** | Every sale in the year with price, postage, fees, item cost and refund flag |
| **Refunds** | Refunds paid in the year |
| **Stock purchases** | Stock paid for in the year |
| **Expenses** | Every expense with its SA103 category and whether a receipt is stored |
| **Unsold stock** | Business items still in stock, at cost |

Totals are real Excel formulas, so they update if anything is edited in the spreadsheet.

> The download only covers **business** figures. Personal sales are in the app but not in the tax summary.
