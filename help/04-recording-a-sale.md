# Recording a sale

![The record a sale form](/guide-img/sale.png)

## Steps

1. Tap **+ Record a sale** on the dashboard, or **Record sale** on an item's page (which picks the item for you).
2. Choose the **Item**. Only items that are *In stock* or *Listed* are shown; personal ones say *(personal)*.
3. **Sale date**: the day the buyer paid. This decides the tax year.
4. **Sold on**: Vinted, eBay or Other. If the item was marked as listed somewhere, that's filled in for you.
5. Fill in the money, then tap **Save sale**. The item becomes **Sold**.

## The money boxes

| Box | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Sale price (£)** | What the buyer paid for the item |
| **Postage charged to buyer (£)** | Postage the buyer paid *to her*. This counts as turnover. |
| **Platform fee (£)** | Fees the platform took from her |
| **Postage she paid (£)** | What she spent sending it |

> **Vinted:** the buyer normally pays postage and the buyer-protection fee to Vinted, not to her, so those boxes usually stay at **0**.
>
> **eBay:** the fee is shown on the order. Easier still, import the eBay report (see *Importing eBay sales*).

## After saving

The item page shows the sale and, for business items, the **profit on this sale**: price plus postage charged, minus fees, postage paid and what the item cost.

The **Sales** page lists every sale in a tax year. Use the year drop-down at the top to switch years.
