# Importing eBay sales

Instead of typing each eBay sale in, download eBay's report and import it. Fees and postage labels come in too.

## 1. Download the report from eBay

On a computer at ebay.co.uk:

1. Go to **Seller Hub** (My eBay → Selling).
2. Click the **Payments** tab.
3. In the left menu, choose **Reports**.
4. Choose **Transaction report**, pick the dates (for a tax year: **6 April to 5 April**) and download it as **CSV**.

> Don't open and re-save the file in Excel first. Excel can change the dates.

## 2. Upload it

1. Go to **Sales → Import eBay CSV**.
2. Choose the file and tap **Check file**. Nothing is saved yet.

![Uploading the eBay report](/guide-img/import-upload.png)

## 3. Check the preview and choose items

Each eBay order is shown with its date, price, postage, fees and any postage label. For each one choose:

- an **existing item**: picked for you if the listing's *Custom label* holds the app's item number;
- **➕ Create new business item**: for past stock sales not in the app yet. You can add **Cost £** and **Date paid**; that's saved as a stock purchase so the cost counts in the right year. Leave it blank if the item came in a bundle you'll record separately.
- **➕ Create new personal item**: for her own clothes;
- **Don't import**: to skip it.

The **Create business items** / **Create personal items** buttons fill every order that has nothing chosen yet.

![Choosing items on the import preview](/guide-img/import-preview.png)

## 4. Import

Tap **Import selected orders** **once**. The button locks while it works, then the **Dashboard** opens on the tax year of those sales with an **eBay import complete** message.

## Good to know

- **Orders already imported are skipped**, so it's safe to upload the same report again.
- Refund lines and other fees (like promoted listings) in the report are **not** imported. Record refunds on the sale, and add promoted listings as an expense.
- **Tip for new listings:** put the app's item number (e.g. `123`) in the eBay listing's **Custom label (SKU)**. Future imports then match automatically.
- Each sale's **tax year comes from its date** in the report (6 April to 5 April).
