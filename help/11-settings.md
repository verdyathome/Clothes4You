# Settings: name, colours, icon and login screen

Open **Settings** from the **☰ menu** on a phone, or the **⚙ Settings** button on a computer. **Only Admins** can see and change Settings.

![The settings page](/guide-img/settings.png)

## App name

Shown top-left and in the browser tab. Up to 40 characters.

## Colour scheme

1. Tap a preset to start from: **Plum**, **Navy**, **Forest**, **Blush**, **Charcoal** or **Dark**.
2. Fine-tune any colour: buttons, button text, page background, panels, header bar and text.
3. The **Preview** updates as you go. Nothing changes until you tap **Save settings**.

If a combination would be hard to read (for example white text on a pale button), a **Readability** warning explains which colours clash.

## App icon

Upload any photo or logo (JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 15 MB). It's **cropped to a square and resized automatically**, so use one with the important part in the middle. It appears in the browser tab, on the login screen, on the launch screen and as the home-screen icon.

With no icon uploaded, a **coat hanger** in your button colour is used. Tick **Remove my icon** to go back to it.

## Login screen background

1. Under **Login screen background**, tap **Choose photo** and pick a picture, for example clothes on a rail.
2. Move the **Darkness** slider until the login box stands out clearly. The small preview shows the effect.
3. Tap **Save settings**.

Big phone photos are **shrunk automatically**. Tick **Remove the photo** to go back to a plain background.

## Use it like an app on the iPhone

1. Open the app in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

From then on, tapping the icon opens the app **full-screen** (no Safari address bar), with a **launch screen** in your page colour and icon while it starts.

> After changing the icon or colours, delete the home-screen icon and add it again: iPhones keep the old icon until you do.

## Start again

**Reset to defaults** puts the name and colours back to the originals: tap it **twice** to confirm (the first tap turns it red). The app icon and login photo are kept.
