# Settings: name, colours and app icon

Open **Settings** from the **☰ menu** on a phone, or the **⚙ Settings** button on a computer.

![The settings page](/guide-img/settings.png)

## App name

Shown top-left and in the browser tab. Up to 40 characters.

## Colour scheme

1. Tap a preset to start from: **Plum**, **Navy**, **Forest**, **Blush**, **Charcoal** or **Dark**.
2. Fine-tune any colour: buttons, button text, page background, panels, header bar and text.
3. The **Preview** updates as you go. Nothing changes until you tap **Save settings**.

If a combination would be hard to read (for example white text on a pale button), a **Readability** warning explains which colours clash.

## App icon

Upload a square image (ideally a 180 × 180 PNG, under 45 KB). It's used in the browser tab and as the icon when the app is added to an iPhone home screen.

> **Add to iPhone home screen:** open the app in Safari, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Start again

**Reset to defaults** puts the name and colours back to the originals. The icon is kept.
