# The platform reporting check

Since 2024, online marketplaces send HMRC details of sellers who make **30 or more sales, or about £1,700 or more**, in a **calendar year** (January to December). That includes sales of personal items.

The table at the bottom of the dashboard shows, per platform for the chosen calendar year:

![The platform reporting check](/guide-img/platform.png)

- **Sales**: number of sales.
- **Gross**: everything buyers paid, including postage charged.
- **Refunds**: refunds paid in that calendar year.
- **Business** and **Personal**: the split of the gross.

A row turns **yellow** once a platform reaches 30 sales or £1,700, the point where it reports to HMRC.

## Why it's useful

If Vinted or eBay tells Kate it has shared her details with HMRC, these totals should roughly match what they sent. The **Personal** column explains the difference between what the platform reported and the turnover on her tax return.

> Use the drop-down on the right of this section to switch calendar year. It's separate from the tax year at the top of the page.
