# Tax years and the SA103 figures

The dashboard shows the figures for the **Self Assessment self-employment pages (SA103)**, using the **cash basis**: money counts when it's paid or received.

![The SA103 section of the dashboard](/guide-img/sa103.png)

## Tax years

A UK tax year runs from **6 April to 5 April**. The drop-down next to the title switches between years.

| Record | Counts in the tax year of… |
|---|---|
| Sale | its **sale date** |
| Refund | the date the refund was **paid** |
| Stock purchase | the **date paid** |
| Expense | the **date paid** |

The return for a tax year is due online by **31 January** after it ends. For example, 2025-26 is due by 31 January 2027.

## What the numbers mean

- **Turnover**: business sale prices plus postage charged to buyers, minus refunds. Personal items are left out.
- **Cost of goods bought for resale**: stock purchases paid for in the year.
- **Other allowable business expenses**: includes platform fees.
- **Phone, fax, stationery and other office costs**: includes postage she paid on sales.
- The other categories come from **Expenses**.
- **Net profit**: turnover minus all the expenses.

The **Stock & item profit** box is for her own interest: unsold stock at cost, and profit per item. It isn't part of the tax return.

> These figures are worked out from what's been entered. Check them with an accountant or HMRC's guidance before filing.
