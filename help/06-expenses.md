# Expenses and receipts

Expenses are the business's running costs. They reduce the profit she pays tax on, so it's worth recording every one.

> **Not an expense here:** stock bought to resell (use a **Stock purchase**), and fees or postage on a particular sale (those go on the sale).

![The add an expense form](/guide-img/expense.png)

## Steps

1. Tap **+ Expense** on the dashboard (or **Expenses → + Expense**).
2. **Date paid**: decides the tax year.
3. **What for?**: pick the closest type. The app shows which SA103 category it goes in.
4. **Description**: e.g. *100 grey mailing bags*.
5. **Amount (£)**, **Supplier**, **Paid by**.
6. **Receipt photo or PDF**: on a phone this can open the camera. Up to 10 MB.
7. Tap **Save expense**, or **Save and add another** to carry straight on.

## Mileage

Choose **Mileage (45p per mile)** and the amount box changes to **Business miles**. Enter the miles for business trips only, for example driving to charity shops to buy stock or to the Post Office. The app works out the claim at **45p a mile**.

> Use mileage *instead of* fuel receipts, not as well.

## Expense types

| Type | Goes in SA103 category |
|---|---|
| Packaging, platform subscriptions, equipment, other | Other allowable business expenses |
| Postage (not linked to a sale), phone/internet share, printing/stationery | Phone, fax, stationery and other office costs |
| Mileage, parking/public transport | Car, van and travel expenses |
| Listing boosts, advertising | Advertising and business entertainment costs |
| PayPal/bank/card charges | Bank, credit card and other financial charges |
| Accountant/software | Accountancy, legal and professional fees |
| Cleaning/repairs to stock | Cost of goods bought for resale |

## Receipts

Open an expense to **View receipt**, or to add or replace one later.

HMRC expects records to be kept for **at least 5 years after the 31 January filing deadline** for that tax year. Receipts stored here count.
